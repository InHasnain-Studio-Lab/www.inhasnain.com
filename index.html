The story

Independent, on purpose.

  1. 01

    Software you own

    I build this way because the software I relied on kept turning into a subscription, and work I had already paid for stopped being mine. Nothing I make is rented. You buy an app once, it runs on your own hardware, and it keeps working whether or not this studio is still here.

  2. 02

    Your machine, not a server

    Most software now sends your files to someone else's computer to do the work, and asks for an account and a monthly fee for the privilege. My apps do the work where your files already are. Nothing is uploaded, I collect nothing, and most of them keep going with the network switched off.

  3. 03

    You deal with the person who built it

    The studio is founder-led and answers to no investors. The person who reads your email is the person responsible for the app, so a bug report reaches the one who can fix it, and every new app has to earn its place next to the ones already there.

  4. 04

    Hasnain Studio X

    What began as single apps is now a studio of 73, in three suites: System, AI Studio and Creative. They are sold through the Microsoft Store and Google Play, so you get the same refunds, updates and checks as any other app there, and HSX Apps Hub keeps the ones you own in one window.

  5. 05

    What comes next

    More of the same, done better: more work that used to need the cloud brought back onto your own device, existing apps kept current for the people who already bought them, and the same rules applied to everything new. Bought once, private, and yours.

Principles

Four rules every app follows.

Runs on your device

The work happens on your own hardware. No uploads to a server you do not control, and no waiting on someone else's queue.

Private by design

No analytics, no account, and nothing collected by me. What you make with the app stays on your device. The free Android versions show Google ads, which the Pro unlock removes.

Pay once

A fair price, paid once, through the store you already trust. Windows apps start with a free trial, and Android apps are free with an optional one-time Pro unlock. No subscriptions.

Sold only through official stores

Every app is bought through the Microsoft Store or Google Play, never a download link on a website, so you get their security checks, automatic updates and refund rules.

Short bio

Hasnain Butt Akhtar is a UK-based software developer and the founder of Hasnain Studio X, an independent studio that designs, builds and publishes Windows and Android apps. All 73 apps run on the user's own device, the studio collects no personal data from the people who use them, and none is sold by subscription.

Long bio

Hasnain Butt Akhtar is an independent software developer in England and the founder of Hasnain Studio X, an independent, founder-led studio publishing Windows and Android software. The studio has released 73 applications across three suites: System, AI Studio and Creative. They are sold through the Microsoft Store and Google Play.

The studio builds against the direction most software has taken. Its apps process files on the user's own device rather than on a server, the studio collects no personal data and asks for no account, and nothing is sold by subscription. HSX Apps Hub, free on the Microsoft Store, gathers the apps a person owns into a single window. Hasnain Studio X is a trade mark of Hasnain Studio X, with a UK trade mark application filed in August 2026.
