Principles

Four rules every app follows.

Runs on your device

The work happens on your own hardware. No uploads to a server you do not control, and no waiting on someone else's queue.

Private by design

No tracking, no analytics, no account needed. What you make with the app stays on your machine.

Pay once

A fair price, paid once, through the store you already trust. No subscriptions, and a free trial first.

Made by one person

When you write in, the person who reads it is the person who built the app. That is how problems get fixed quickly.
