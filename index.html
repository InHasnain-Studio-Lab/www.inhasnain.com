The story

Built alone, on purpose.

  1. 01

    Software you own

    I build this way because the software I relied on kept turning into a subscription, and work I had already paid for stopped being mine. Nothing I make is rented. You buy an app once, it runs on your own hardware, and it keeps working whether or not this studio is still here.

  2. 02

    Your machine, not a server

    Most software now sends your files to someone else's computer to do the work, and asks for an account and a monthly fee for the privilege. My apps do the work where your files already are. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and most of them keep going with the network switched off.

  3. 03

    One developer, every part

    There is no team here. The code, the interface, the icon, the Store listing, the privacy policy, this website and the reply to your email are all mine. It is slower than having a team, and it means every app has to earn its place, but nothing gets lost between departments.

  4. 04

    Hasnain Studio X

    What began as single apps is now a studio of 72, in three suites: System, AI Studio and Creative. They are sold through the Microsoft Store and Google Play, so you get the same refunds, updates and checks as any other app there, and HSX Apps Hub keeps the ones you own in one window.

  5. 05

    What comes next

    More of the same, done better: more work that used to need the cloud brought back onto your own device, existing apps kept current for the people who already bought them, and the same rules applied to everything new. Bought once, private, and yours.

Principles

Four rules every app follows.

Runs on your device

The work happens on your own hardware. No uploads to a server you do not control, and no waiting on someone else's queue.

Private by design

No tracking, no analytics, no account needed. What you make with the app stays on your machine.

Pay once

A fair price, paid once, through the store you already trust. No subscriptions, and a free trial first.

Made by one person

When you write in, the person who reads it is the person who built the app. That is how problems get fixed quickly.

Short bio

Hasnain Butt Akhtar is a UK-based software developer and the founder of Hasnain Studio X, an independent studio that designs, builds and publishes Windows and Android apps. All 72 apps run on the user's own device, collect no personal data and are sold once, with no subscription.

Long bio

Hasnain Butt Akhtar is an independent software developer in England and the founder of Hasnain Studio X, a one-person studio publishing Windows and Android software. Working alone on every part, from code and design to support, the studio has released 72 applications across three suites: System, AI Studio and Creative. They are sold through the Microsoft Store and Google Play.

The studio builds against the direction most software has taken. Its apps process files on the user's own device rather than on a server, collect no personal data and need no account, and are bought once instead of by subscription. HSX Apps Hub, free on the Microsoft Store, gathers the apps a person owns into a single window. Hasnain Studio X is a trade mark of Hasnain Studio X, with a UK trade mark application filed in August 2026.
