What this site collects

Nothing. There is no account, no form, no analytics and no cookie, and nothing is stored in your browser.

What your browser still sends

Like any website, these pages reach you through a hosting and delivery service, which receives the technical details every request carries, such as your IP address, the page asked for and your browser version, and keeps them under its own policy for security. The typefaces come from a web font service, which receives the same details when your browser asks for them. No cookie is set.

The list of apps, their artwork and the latest releases are read from hasnainstudiox.com, the studio's own site. Its website privacy policy covers it.

Links and email
